Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31/07/2026

Zaar Data Labs ("we," "our," or "us") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you interact with our provider profile and data products on the Snowflake Data Marketplace.

1. The Nature of Our Data Products

100% Synthetic Datasets: We specialize in generating and providing synthetic cold-chain logistics data, including simulated telemetry, spatial, and odometry records. The datasets we publish and sell on the Snowflake Data Marketplace contain no real-world personal data, personally identifiable information (PII), or protected health information (PHI).

2. Information We Collect

While our data products contain no personal information, we may collect limited business contact information when you interact with us as a consumer on the Snowflake Data Marketplace. This includes:

  • Account Information: Your name, corporate email address, company name, and job title when you request access to our personalized datasets, subscribe to a product, or contact us for support.
  • Usage Metrics: Standard telemetry provided by the Snowflake platform regarding your query volume and interaction with our shared data shares.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the business contact information collected exclusively to:

  • Fulfill data access requests and provision secure data shares via Snowflake.
  • Provide technical support and respond to your inquiries.
  • Notify you of schema changes, data refreshes, or updates to our logistics data factory.
  • Monitor marketplace usage to ensure compliance with our licensing terms.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With cloud infrastructure providers (such as AWS and Snowflake) that host our data factory and facilitate our marketplace transactions, strictly for the purpose of delivering our services.
  • Legal Compliance: If required to do so by law, or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your business contact information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to consumer data is strictly limited to authorized personnel managing our Snowflake Provider Profile.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. The updated version will be indicated by the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
